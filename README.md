## Before you start

- **Internet connection:** The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D library from a public content delivery network, so an internet connection is needed for them to look right.
- **Browser:** Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The hardware page shows 3D models, so your browser needs WebGL support (most modern browsers have it).
- **Screen size:** The pages are designed for a laptop or desktop screen. They also work on a tablet, but the layout is tighter on a phone.

## How to use the pages

- **Speaker software:** Scroll and click through the screens to see how a child moves through the interface.
- **Speaker hardware:** Drag to rotate the 3D view and use the controls on the page to look at each part. Use the step-by-step section to see how the parts fit together.
